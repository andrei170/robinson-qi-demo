Why it costs what it costs

Every tree is priced on site

“How much to take a tree down?” depends on what is around it. These are the things that move the price, and why we look before we quote.

Power lines

Lines near the tree change how it comes down, and sometimes who has to be called first.

Over a house or barn

A tree over a structure comes down piece by piece, not in one drop.

Wells and gas lines

What is underground decides where the wood can land.

Open ground

A tree in an open field with room to drop costs far less.
